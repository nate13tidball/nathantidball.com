# TOBEWES portfolio reference

Source inspected: [tobennawes.com](https://tobennawes.com/)

## Observed structure

- A persistent header uses a small personal brand and short section links.
- The home page opens with a large identity-led hero, followed by short, paced
  statement sections that frame the person and their work.
- Featured projects use a repeated list of concise title, year, summary, and
  project link.
- A career timeline follows projects, then a contact prompt and a footer with
  professional and social links.
- Separate About, Blog, and Resume destinations are available from the header.

## Adaptation on NathanTidball.com

The homepage borrows the information hierarchy: identity first, About, selected
projects, experience, then contact and social links. It uses a distinct
royal-blue, white, and silver palette, a responsive editorial grid, and content
focused on Nathan's engineering and research history. No source HTML, styles,
assets, or prose were reused.

Captured 2026-11-12 from the publicly served page source. Browser UI inspection
was unavailable in the Codex session, so the observations are limited to its
server-rendered page structure and visible text content.
