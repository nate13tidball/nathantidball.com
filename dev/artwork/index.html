Small pieces. Big picture.

Made for exploring large artwork.

The viewer starts with small tiles and requests more detail only where you look. Your browser never needs to download or decode the entire original just to open the image.

This sample has 142 million pixels; it is not a gigabyte-file benchmark. The same tile-based approach is intended for the much larger artwork to come. Smoothness still depends on the tile host, connection, and device.

Try it out
  1. Let the overview appear, then choose 1:1 detail to inspect the masonry.
  2. Drag to a different area. Watch the loaded tile count increase as detail arrives.
  3. Choose Fit image, then try fullscreen and keyboard navigation.
  4. Try it on your phone. Pinch inside the image; scroll the page outside it.

The first-tile timer measures one drawn tile, not a fully sharp viewport. The counter records tile-load events, not network bytes or total memory.
